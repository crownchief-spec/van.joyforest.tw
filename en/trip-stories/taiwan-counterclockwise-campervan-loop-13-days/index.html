Taiwan Counterclockwise Campervan Loop | 13 Days and 12 Nights

A practical 13-day, 12-night Taiwan campervan loop for first-time international visitors: collect the van at Taoyuan Airport, travel south along the west, return north on the east coast and hand it back in Taipei.

2026-08-29 · About 18 minutes ·Taiwan campervan itinerary, 13 days 12 nights, counterclockwise Taiwan loop, Taoyuan Airport campervan, Taipei return, Taiwan road trip
中文

Our clear recommendation: 13 days and 12 nights

For a first campervan loop around Taiwan, our recommended length is 13 days and 12 nights. It gives Sun Moon Lake, Alishan, Xiaoliuqiu, Kenting and the east coast enough room without turning every day into a long drive. Collect the campervan at Taoyuan International Airport, travel counterclockwise down the west side, return north along the Pacific and hand the vehicle back in Taipei on Day 13. Taipei itself is easier by MRT, taxi or a private driver than in a campervan approximately three metres high.

This is a baseline itinerary, not a compulsory tour package. The framework is fixed at 13 days and 12 nights, while individual activities can move with flight times, season, interests and energy. Reconfirm campsite bookings, ferries, mountain roads, weather and attraction access shortly before departure.

Day 1 | Taoyuan Airport collection, supplies and an easy first coastal night

After the campervan handover, equipment check and driving briefing at Taoyuan International Airport, avoid a long drive on the first day. Stop at PX MART (全聯福利中心) for water, food, breakfast and snacks. Many coastal stops and rural campsites have few nearby shops, while the campervan has a refrigerator, so buying supplies first makes the journey much easier.

  • Taoyuan International Airport: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Taoyuan+International+Airport
  • PX MART: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=PX+Mart+Taoyuan
  • Xucuogang Seaside Cottage (許厝港海邊小屋): https://maps.app.goo.gl/TLTrAxuG93oyFC1a6?g_st=ic

Xucuogang is close enough to the airport for a sunset and a gentle first evening with the vehicle. A scenic parking area is not automatically a licensed campsite: follow on-site signs and local rules, confirm whether overnight parking is allowed, and do not set out furniture, cook or occupy additional spaces in a normal car park.

Day 2 | Miaoli Cape Paradise and Feng Chia Night Market in Taichung

Follow the west coast south to Cape Paradise in Houlong, Miaoli, with sea views, wind turbines and an open sunset. It may work as a rest or overnight candidate, but nearby food is limited, so arrive with supplies. Continue into Taichung for street food at Feng Chia Night Market, then stay at a proper coastal campsite.

  • Cape Paradise, Miaoli (海角樂園): https://maps.app.goo.gl/tUaW3LAEc4MSAi3u8?g_st=ic
  • Feng Chia Night Market: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Feng+Chia+Night+Market
  • Xiang Hai Na Yang / Daan Coastal Campground (向海那漾): https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Xiang+Hai+Na+Yang+Campground+Taichung

Day 3 | Historic Lukang and a lakeside night at Sun Moon Lake

Visit Lukang Tianhou Temple and the old streets in Changhua. Temples, traditional shop houses, snacks and souvenirs are all within a walkable area and offer an accessible introduction to Taiwanese culture. Continue to Sun Moon Lake in the afternoon for the Xiangshan cycling route, a boat ride or a relaxed lakeside meal.

  • Lukang Tianhou Temple: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Lukang+Tianhou+Temple
  • Xiangshan Visitor Center, Sun Moon Lake: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Xiangshan+Visitor+Center+Sun+Moon+Lake
  • Tiepoka Campground (帖泊喀露營區): https://maps.app.goo.gl/WG8tYhhvLkRoqhRJA?g_st=ic

Tiepoka is notable for pitches close to the lake. Confirm availability, vehicle height and length, power, lakefront pitch type and check-in time directly with the campsite before arrival.

Day 4 | A slow Sun Moon Lake morning, then Alishan before sunset

Keep the morning for cycling or a boat trip, then leave around midday for Alishan. This is a deliberate detour into the mountains rather than a naturally direct part of the coastal loop. It involves a long, winding drive, but sunset, cloud seas, stars, the sunrise railway and forest trails can justify the effort. Aim to arrive before sunset and never force a late-night mountain drive.

  • Alishan National Forest Recreation Area: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Alishan+National+Forest+Recreation+Area
  • Official Alishan Forest Railway information: https://afrch.forest.gov.tw/0000300
Alishan overnight note: the National Forest Recreation Area is not a free-camping zone. Use legal hotel or guesthouse accommodation, or pre-book a licensed campervan-compatible campground outside the recreation area. The Zhushan sunrise-train timetable is normally announced the previous afternoon; check and purchase tickets in advance.

Day 5 | Alishan sunrise and forest, then Guanziling hot springs

A sunrise morning may start around 4 a.m. After sunrise, walk forest trails back toward the main visitor area. Eat lunch and rest in the vehicle before descending; do not begin a winding downhill drive while exhausted. Guanziling's distinctive mud hot springs make a restorative stop after the early start and mountain road.

  • Reikei Hot Spring Resort (儷景溫泉會館), hotel option: https://maps.app.goo.gl/h4dP8N5JA3VopcoP8?g_st=ic
  • Sen Spa Water Park (森之湯脈衝 SPA 親子水樂園), confirm the current vehicle-stay arrangement: https://maps.app.goo.gl/woyCFgxGKcYhkDnj6?g_st=ic

Day 6 | Tainan food and heritage, Kaohsiung harbour culture, then Donggang

Tainan has too many worthwhile places and foods to collect in one short visit. Choose one main area—Tainan Confucius Temple, or Anping Fort and its old streets—and leave time for snacks, temples and walking. Continue to Kaohsiung and select either the striking Weiwuying arts centre or the Hamasen Railway Cultural Park beside the harbour. Move to Donggang or Dapeng Bay for the night so the next morning's ferry is easy.

  • Tainan Confucius Temple: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tainan+Confucius+Temple
  • Anping Fort: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Anping+Fort
  • National Kaohsiung Center for the Arts, Weiwuying: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=National+Kaohsiung+Center+for+the+Arts+Weiwuying
  • Hamasen Railway Cultural Park: https://maps.app.goo.gl/esTybNZ9bEeKhttq8?g_st=ic
  • Changdi Campground (長堤露營區), a backup between Tainan and Kaohsiung; confirm campervan access: https://maps.app.goo.gl/1DAy3bgAkRhoR1TD8?g_st=ic

Day 7 | A sea-turtle day on Xiaoliuqiu, then Kenting

Leave the campervan at an operated or explicitly approved parking area in Donggang and take an early ferry to Xiaoliuqiu. The island is compact and can be explored by scooter or electric scooter. Its signature experience is guided snorkeling or diving with sea turtles. Use a legal operator, never touch or chase wildlife, and keep a respectful distance. Return on a late-afternoon ferry and continue to Kenting.

  • Donggang ferry terminal for Xiaoliuqiu: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Donggang+Ferry+Terminal+Xiaoliuqiu
  • Xiaoliuqiu: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Xiaoliuqiu
  • KLOOK: https://www.klook.com/
  • KKday: https://www.kkday.com/

Day 8 | A full Kenting day: water, the aquarium or Taiwan's southern coast

Kenting deserves two nights, so do not move camp on Day 8. The National Museum of Marine Biology and Aquarium requires at least half a day. Snorkeling, diving, water sports and boat trips can be reserved through KLOOK or KKday. Hengchun town is the convenient place for food and supplies. Choose only two or three main themes instead of attempting everything.

  • National Museum of Marine Biology and Aquarium: https://maps.app.goo.gl/vsy91NaBHbxYsPtx8?g_st=ic
  • Houbi Lake Intertidal Zone (後壁湖潮間帶): https://maps.app.goo.gl/XBw8juFCuJ86bXUx8?g_st=ic
  • Happy Baisha Campground (快樂白沙露營區): https://maps.app.goo.gl/rJ5USBJfT6Zk2WuV6?g_st=ic
  • Bengang Live Seafood (本港活海鮮): https://maps.app.goo.gl/d3kdcrMjSJXQ4pnL7?g_st=ic
  • Eluanbi Lighthouse: https://maps.app.goo.gl/Uo7J5w9gk6kWByJ27?g_st=ic
  • Sand Island car park (砂島停車場): https://maps.app.goo.gl/Nrcv2DwashW39KPH7?g_st=ic

At low tide, Houbi Lake may reveal starfish, sea urchins and small fish. Wear non-slip footwear, carry a torch, do not enter alone, and decide according to that night's tides, waves and access rules. Sand Island and other coastal car parks are scenic stop candidates, not automatic overnight campsites.

Day 9 | Kenting through the South Link coast to Taitung

Travelers who surf can stop around Jialeshui in Manzhou before following the long South Link route toward Taitung. This is a winding mountain-and-sea drive, so the main goal is the scenery and a safe arrival rather than another long attraction list. Walk around the Tiehua Music Village area in the evening, then return to a pre-booked legal campsite around Taitung.

  • Jialeshui Scenic Area: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Jialeshui+Scenic+Area
  • Tiehua Music Village: https://maps.app.goo.gl/GhEJV8W5VxdSFF766?g_st=ic

Day 10 | Taitung, Luye, Chishang and Sanxiantai to Hualien

In summer, check the current year's balloon programme at Luye Highlands; paragliding may also be available by reservation. Continue to Chishang for a railway lunchbox, then travel north along the east coast through Sanxiantai. Late-summer daylily flowers can be a seasonal option, but bloom dates and traffic controls change yearly. Taitung to Hualien is long: reduce stops when daylight or energy is running short.

  • Wu-Tao Chishang Lunchbox Cultural Museum: https://maps.app.goo.gl/UKQek8zDoFnotjt39?g_st=ic
  • Sanxiantai: https://maps.app.goo.gl/8zfxTTeyZ7qz1oA47?g_st=ic
  • Hualien Ocean Campground (花蓮海洋露營區): https://maps.app.goo.gl/ZUYG6Ux5kFsCMQax5?g_st=ic

Day 11 | Hualien City, Qixingtan and the mountain-and-sea landscape of Chongde

If the previous evening's arrival was early, visit Dongdamen Night Market. On Day 11, walk beside the Pacific and pebble beach at Qixingtan, leaving the stones where they belong. Continue to Chongde in the afternoon and keep the night for the coast north of Hualien. The access road to Banxia Beach is narrow; if vehicle width, weather or road conditions are unsuitable, choose an easier Chongde coastal stop. Join SUP or sunrise trips only with a qualified local operator.

  • Hualien Dongdamen Night Market: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Hualien+Dongdamen+Night+Market
  • Qixingtan Scenic Area: https://maps.app.goo.gl/RvmMyatkykwD6LbJ9?g_st=ic
  • Na Zuoshan Yinyin (那座山隱癮): https://maps.app.goo.gl/v5EYYvqBCmrtecDo6?g_st=ic
  • Banxia Beach (坂下海灘): https://maps.app.goo.gl/5ocXsgkbCTmWpsSJ9?g_st=ic

Day 12 | Qingshui Cliff, Nan'ao and a Yilan hot-spring evening

Start at the Chongde Rest Area for a Qingshui Cliff view, then follow the latest official traffic guidance along the Suhua corridor toward Yilan. An ATV beach experience around Nan'ao can be booked in advance; otherwise, keep the time for Yilan. Finish with a Jiaoxi hot spring, food at Luodong Night Market, or one night in one of Yilan's distinctive guesthouses.

  • Chongde Rest Area: https://maps.app.goo.gl/Sc7pVL8FqQ5CXaF87?g_st=ic
  • Legend Hot Spring Bathhouse (傳奇溫泉湯屋), confirm vehicle-stay and bathing fees: https://maps.app.goo.gl/iUyKbd9fGy9s4Zbo8?g_st=ic
  • Honeymoon Bay: https://maps.app.goo.gl/cwBZ3MS7AfSY4bcd8?g_st=ic
  • Luodong Night Market: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Luodong+Night+Market

Honeymoon Bay is suitable for a seaside stop. Whether a vehicle may remain overnight depends on current signs, land management and local rules; a beach car park must not be assumed to permit camping.

Day 13 | Yilan to the Taipei vehicle return

Do not add a long Northeast Coast attraction on the final day. Keep margin for traffic, packing, equipment checks and the agreed return appointment. After returning the campervan in Taipei, use the MRT or a taxi for Taipei 101, Din Tai Fung and other city sights. Jiufen, Pingxi and Yehliu are farther away and work better as a separate private-driver or shuttle day after the vehicle return.

  • Taipei 101: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Taipei+101
  • Taipei Metro network: https://english.metro.taipei/

Essential campervan notes for this loop

  • Legal overnight locations: Use Taiwan's official campsite information platform as a starting point: https://camp.tad.gov.tw/ A seaside attraction, car park or rest area is not automatically a campsite or an approved overnight location.
  • Vehicle height: The campervan is about three metres high. Do not enter underground car parks, low canopies or entrances without clearly sufficient clearance.
  • Long descents and brakes: Select a lower gear and use engine braking before the descent; do not ride the brake pedal continuously. If you smell overheating, the pedal feels soft or braking seems abnormal, stop immediately in a safe place. Allow at least 15–20 minutes to cool and reassess the vehicle. Never pour water onto hot brake discs. If braking remains abnormal, do not continue driving.
  • Seat-belt safety: While moving, every passenger must remain in one of the six approved seats with a seat belt. Nobody may ride on a bed, in the aisle or at the rear of the living area.
  • Weather and roads: Typhoons, monsoon wind, heavy rain, rockfall and construction can alter the route. Recheck Alishan, the South Link, the Hualien–Taitung coast and the Suhua corridor shortly before travel.

How to use this template

Keep the 13-day, 12-night framework, the daily regions and the overnight areas as the clear structure, then exchange activities according to each guest. Ocean lovers keep Xiaoliuqiu and Kenting; travelers who dislike very early mornings can skip the Alishan sunrise; anyone seeking a gentler pace should remove activities rather than compress the whole trip. The structure lets guests and host immediately understand which day is being discussed—even while details are still changing.
