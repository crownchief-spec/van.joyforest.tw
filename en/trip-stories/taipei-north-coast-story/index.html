Pickup at Taipei 101 → North Coast overnight & morning light

From Xinyi pickup along the North Coast: seafood dinner, stars and surf at night, coastal dawn through the window—three photos tying city and sea in one short trip.

Trip story｜Taipei 101 pickup to North Coast dinner, overnight, and morning light

Want city night views and coastal sunrise in one trip? Three photos sketch a very believable campervan mini-journey: pickup downtown, slow along the North Coast; seafood dinner to close the day; waves as background at night; next morning, roll over and meet sunrise and surf through the window.

① City departure, full ceremony. Handover near Xinyi at dusk, Taipei skyline beside the van—like telling yourself: "For the next few days, home moves with the sea breeze." Stock groceries and drinks on the way; the fridge becomes a mobile pantry so cooking later isn't frantic.

② North Coast seafood dinner, eaten slowly. North along the sea line—harbor snacks, seafood stir-fry, or simple noodles work as "night-one landing ritual." After eating, don't rush; find a legal, comfortable-view coastal spot or campsite, pull out table and chairs, or stay inside with a small light and sort photos to distant waves.

③ Stars and surf—the "late-night room type" only a campervan gives. Coastal nights are often clearer than the city: wind outside the door, familiar bed and bathroom inside. That freedom of **traveling with your room** is why many rent once and stay hooked—the image below is the mood of parking into the sea breeze after dark.

④ Sunrise without fighting for a spot—the window is your frame. Sea brightens first, clouds turn gold-orange; linger in bed, start coffee, then decide whether to walk the sand. Next image: morning light from bed through the window (frame and pillow in shot)—a different angle and mood from Kenting palm aerials.

Note: Parking and overnight rules vary on popular coastal spots—follow signs and law; actual route, stops, and campervan suitability per booking and handover briefing.
