Campervan Beginner Articles

Knowing how to pitch a tent does not mean you know van life—but outdoor etiquette and safety use the same foundation.

中文

Why a Learning Path Instead of One Long Read?

Campervan beginners absorb driving, living onboard, water/electric systems, and outdoor etiquette at once. Without order, it is easy to get lost in details. Start with a rental guide for “what am I booking,” then a how-to cheat sheet for “what to understand on board,” then pricing and routes.

From Tent Camping to Campervan: What Stays, What Changes

Tent camping builds tarp lines, campsite courtesy, trash handling, and fire awareness—all still apply in a campervan. What changes most is living inside the vehicle and fixed water/power/waste systems, which require reading orientation material and following campsite rules.

Role of Official Resources

The Ministry of Transportation and Tourism covers travel safety and sustainability; the Freeway Bureau covers long-distance and urban driving safety; natural sites return to scenic area and forestry authority notices. Treat government information as the baseline and rental orientation as the operating manual—use both.
