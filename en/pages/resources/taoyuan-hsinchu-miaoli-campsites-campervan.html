Why Taoyuan–Hsinchu–Miaoli Works as a “Second Leg”

From Taipei, extending toward Taoyuan, Hsinchu, and Miaoli often lets you switch between urban resupply, hill country, and coast within a day. For campervans, that means confirming early whether pitches allow large vehicles and enough turning space—stress peaks at evening check-in.

How to Use Official Tourism Information

The Taoyuan City Tourism Bureau covers themes like Daxi, Lalashan, and the coast; the Hsinchu County Tourism Website suits Neiwan, Guanxi, and mountain towns; the Miaoli County Culture and Tourism Bureau spans Tai’an, Nanzhuang, and coastal routes. Use these for attraction density—but campervan access still depends on campsite rules.

Forests and Conservation Areas

For forest recreation areas or nature trails, check the Taiwan Forest Recreation portal and on-site notices; some zones restrict vehicle access, fire, and carrying capacity seasonally. Conservation and safety determine whether you can follow your plan as written.
