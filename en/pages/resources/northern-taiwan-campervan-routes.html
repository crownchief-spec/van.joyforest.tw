Northern Taiwan Campervan Routes

Northern Taiwan’s challenge is strong coastal wind and heavy urban traffic—leave slack in the schedule and park legally.

中文

First Rule for Northern Routes: Leave Slack

Whether it is a weekend loop or a 3-day, 2-night trip, greater Taipei and the coast often see weekend traffic and parking pressure. Campervans are wider and need more time to park, turn, and open doors—build buffer into the route instead of packing every attraction. The Ministry of Transportation and Tourism also encourages safety and comfort over over-packed itineraries.

Role of the North Coast National Scenic Area

The North Coast and Guanyinshan National Scenic Area Administration publishes attraction and recreation information and updates carrying capacity and safety reminders. In strong wind, secure awnings and tarps per safety guidance; when photographing, watch tide pools and wave conditions underfoot.

Urban Access and Freeways

Entering and leaving freeways from Taipei and New Taipei, review Freeway Bureau ramp and alternate route information first; rest on schedule for long drives. If large-vehicle driving is new to you, Highway Bureau YouTube safety videos help establish basics.
