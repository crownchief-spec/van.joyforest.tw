Campervan How-To Cheat Sheet: Water, Drainage & Power

Break campervan water/drainage and electrical basics into “concepts first, steps second”—then follow our official how-to page step by step.

中文

Water fill & drainage: know the locations, then the rhythm

Most first-timers worry about “doing it wrong,” but once you know where the fresh tank, fill port, and drain valves are—and match that to your shower rhythm—a 3-day/2-night trip usually goes smoothly. For full step photos and videos, open our campervan how-to guide page, which covers driving height, power, and tank operations.

Power: A/C, fridge, and campsite hookup priorities

The most common campervan electrical question is “how long can I run the A/C?” To avoid duplication, full panel reading (volts left / amps center / watts right) lives on the official campervan how-to guide page. Key idea here: when shore power is available, charge the batteries first; run high-draw appliances during times you can recharge—that keeps the trip calmer.

Why treat the how-to page as a pre-departure checklist

A text cheat sheet builds concepts; the real work happens at handover and on the road. Think of this beginner campervan guide as “preview,” and the in-person briefing as “practice”—together they prevent last-minute panic.
