01

Driving, cameras & keys

  1. The campervan is about 3 m high. Avoid underground car parks, low awnings, signs and branches. If clearance is uncertain, stop and check or take another route.
  2. The body extends behind the rear wheels and swings outward in a turn. Turn and reverse slowly, leaving extra room at both sides and the right rear corner.
  3. Camera views, left to right: rear-facing, rear overhead and right rear corner. Cameras are aids. Stop and check outside when unsure; use a spotter standing safely clear if needed.
  4. There are 3 front and 3 rear seats in the driving cab. Everyone must use a designated seat and seat belt while moving. Use the lounge and beds only when parked.
  5. Keys in the photo, left to right: driving cab, rear camper door and water cap. Turn the cab key toward the front to unlock and toward the rear to lock. Check each door before leaving.
  6. Secure luggage in unused cab space. For music: Home → Apps → Bluetooth Phone → pair your phone.
Published dimensions are approximately 5 m long and 2 m wide. Check the full vehicle outline when parking and keep traffic and pavements clear. See the four safety cards for downhill and other driving advice.

02

Awning, side table & entry

  1. Confirm that the venue allows awnings, tables and outdoor equipment, then park securely. Follow signs at ordinary parking spaces and keep equipment within permitted areas.
  2. The crank and two support poles are under the rear cab seat. Hook the crank into the awning eye and turn slowly to extend.
  3. Support the awning with the supplied poles and check stability. Retract it in strong wind or unsettled weather, before sleeping or when leaving it unattended.
  4. Use the fold-down side table only when parked and permitted. Before driving, stow the table, awning, supports and crank, and check every latch.
  5. Fully open the folding step on firm, level ground. Remove shoes on the entrance mat. Stow and secure the step before driving.
The awning is not a storm shelter. Extending it only a little does not replace venue permission. Follow site rules for all outdoor equipment.
