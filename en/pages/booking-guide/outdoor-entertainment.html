Awning, side table & entry

  1. Confirm that the venue allows awnings, tables and outdoor equipment, then park securely. Follow signs at ordinary parking spaces and keep equipment within permitted areas.
  2. The crank and two support poles are under the rear cab seat. Hook the crank into the awning eye and turn slowly to extend.
  3. Support the awning with the supplied poles and check stability. Retract it in strong wind or unsettled weather, before sleeping or when leaving it unattended.
  4. Use the fold-down side table only when parked and permitted. Before driving, stow the table, awning, supports and crank, and check every latch.
  5. Fully open the folding step on firm, level ground. Remove shoes on the entrance mat. Stow and secure the step before driving.
The awning is not a storm shelter. Extending it only a little does not replace venue permission. Follow site rules for all outdoor equipment.

Storage, fridge & entertainment

  1. The tall cupboard by the entrance holds the camping table and chairs. Keep the aisle clear and return clean equipment to its original place.
  2. Upper cupboards hold the Switch, cables and projector fittings, followed by picnic fabric, mats, string lights, bedding, sheets and pillowcases.
  3. The cupboards above the fridge hold the picnic basket, Bluetooth speaker, projector, stand and hair dryer. Follow your handover inventory for included items.
  4. The upper fridge compartment is the freezer; the lower one is the fridge. Turn its panel switch on, secure food and latch the door. There is currently no microwave.
  5. Secure the projector on its stand, then connect an HDMI source such as the Switch. USB-C phones must support video output; streaming services may restrict playback. Select HDMI audio or the Bluetooth speaker as appropriate.
  6. Keep electronics and string lights dry. Close and latch each cupboard after use and check them all before driving.
Bring a personal pillow if you prefer a larger one. Ask before using high-power cooking appliances such as rice cookers, induction hobs or air fryers.

How to set up the projector and speaker

Mount the projector on its tripod first. For video, connect your phone’s USB-C port to the projector’s HDMI input. For sound, run a separate 3.5 mm audio cable from the projector’s audio output to the speaker’s AUX input. Tap the diagram or any photo to enlarge it.

Jump to projection screen setup ↓

What you’ll need

  • Projector, tripod, Bluetooth speaker and projection screen.
  • USB-C to HDMI cable for video and a 3.5 mm audio cable for sound.
  • Place the speaker on a stable surface and run cables along the edges, away from walkways.
1

Set up the projector and speaker

Fully extend all three tripod legs and place them on level ground before securing the projector. Put the speaker on a stable table and keep the tripod and cables clear of foot traffic.

2

Connect your phone with USB-C to HDMI

  1. Plug the HDMI end into the projector’s HDMI input (the silver connector on the right of the photo). Plug the USB-C end into your phone.
  2. Unlock the phone. If the picture doesn’t come up automatically, select HDMI as the projector’s input source.
  3. If there’s still no picture, reconnect both ends and check that your device supports video output over USB-C. Some streaming services may restrict playback on an external display.
3

Connect the speaker’s AUX input

  1. Run a 3.5 mm audio cable from the projector’s round audio output to the AUX jack on the back of the speaker.
  2. Turn on the speaker, make sure both plugs are fully inserted and adjust the volume.
  3. If you have a picture but no sound, use the source button indicated by the left arrow in the photo to select AUX (wired input).
4

Mount the projection screen and focus the picture

The lightweight screen is stored on the left-hand side of the upper bed above the cab. Take it out, unfold it and choose a spot and height that suit your campsite. The van comes with tape, hooks and short guy lines, so you can mount the screen in either of these ways.

  • Tape it to the side of the van. Lay the screen flat against the side and secure its edges with the supplied tape.
  • Hang it from the outer edge of the side awning. Use the supplied hooks and short guy lines to hang the screen from secure points at your chosen height. Adjust the lines until the screen hangs flat.

Once the screen is secure, set the projector tripod on level ground and aim the lens at the centre of the screen. Adjust the tripod height, projector distance and focus, then play a short clip to check the picture, sound and subtitles.

Outdoor equipment, use and storage

The outdoor bundle includes tables, chairs, string lights, a tent or canopy-style setup and picnic accessories. The entertainment bundle includes the projector, screen, Bluetooth speaker, Switch and karaoke microphones. Items depend on your booking and stock. See the bundle details and offers.

  • Use the projector, speaker, string lights and cables only in dry conditions and on dry ground. Pack them away if it rains or the ground becomes damp.
  • Set up outdoor equipment only at campsites or venues that allow it. Keep the equipment inside the van in ordinary car parks.
  • After switching off the projector, wait for it to cool before unplugging and packing it. Hold the plugs when disconnecting cables, and put everything back where it was at handover. Damage or loss may be charged at actual cost.
