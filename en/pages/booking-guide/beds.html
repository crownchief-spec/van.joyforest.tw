Dinette bed & fixed upper bed

  1. Park securely and apply the parking brake. Clear the table and put cushions aside. Keep objects away from the table mechanism.
  2. The tabletop moves forward, back and sideways. Centre it so it can lower without catching the seats.
  3. Hold the release control on the table pedestal and guide the top down slowly with both hands, until level with the side supports. Keep fingers clear of moving and pinching points.
  4. Check that the tabletop and supports are stable. Move the seat cushions inward, fill the centre with the narrow cushion and place backrest cushions at the sides to make a flat bed.
  5. The upper bed stays in place. Reverse the process to restore the dinette, supporting the top and raising it as demonstrated. Do not force the mechanism or stand on the table.
Beds are for parked use only. Nobody may travel in the bed or lounge. Restore and secure the table and cushions before driving.
