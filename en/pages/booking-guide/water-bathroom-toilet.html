03

Fresh water, diesel & AdBlue

  1. The fresh-water inlet is the round cap marked WATER on the right side. Unlock it with the water-cap key and use the clean supplied hose with a suitable fresh-water source.
  2. The tank holds about 110 L. Check the indoor gauge first. Stay with the van during filling, watch the level, then stop the water, stow the hose and lock the cap.
  3. At a campsite, ask which tap to use. Elsewhere, ask the fuel-station staff before refuelling whether suitable water is available. Try another refill point if needed.
  4. Do not use unknown wash water or recycled water. Keep the hose clean. Do not treat the onboard tank water as drinking water.
  5. The vehicle takes DIESEL. Its fuel filler is on the right, by the passenger side. Give the cab key to the attendant and request a full tank of diesel.
  6. The blue cap is for AdBlue/DEF, not water or petrol. Diesel, AdBlue and fresh water have different inlets. If unsure, stop and send us a photo.
Putting water into the AdBlue tank can damage the vehicle. Let the fuel attendant handle diesel; refill fresh water only at the confirmed WATER inlet.

07

Toilet, treatment & waste cassette

  1. Once parked, switch on the bathroom light and rotate the toilet to a usable position. Open the blade valve as demonstrated and check the central opening before use.
  2. Flush after use, close the blade valve and toilet lid. Never put wet wipes, sanitary products, plastic or other foreign items into the toilet.
  3. Add treatment after emptying the cassette, not after every toilet visit. The video demonstrates about 100–200 mL; follow the supplied product concentration, label and handover dose.
  4. Close the blade valve before removal. Open the low white service hatch by the entrance, lift the retaining catch and pull the cassette out level. Do not force a stuck cassette.
  5. Empty only at an approved chemical-toilet or waste-disposal point. Turn out the spout, remove its cap, empty and rinse as directed, then close, reinstall and check the retaining catch.
Never empty waste onto roads, soil, storm drains or ordinary sinks. Public toilets may not accept chemical-toilet waste; get the operator’s permission and follow chemical restrictions.

08

Shower, toiletries & blinds

  1. Check fresh water, switch the pump on and open the tap. The bathroom tap pulls out as a shower head. Adjust and test the temperature before showering.
  2. Use a short, stop-start shower: wet, turn water off to wash, then rinse. Use campsite showers when available to conserve the onboard supply.
  3. If hot water fails, temperature is abnormal or the pump runs continuously, close the tap and switch the pump off. Contact us; do not alter gas fittings or wiring.
  4. Towels and disposable toothbrushes are in the cupboard above the bathroom. Close it after use. Dry pooled water, ventilate and switch the pump off before leaving.
  5. Press the grey catch and move the blinds gently: blackout above, insect screen below. Support the rails with both hands. Shut and latch windows before driving.
The fresh-water tank is about 110 L, so avoid long continuous showers. Follow the handover demonstration for hot water and gas appliances.
