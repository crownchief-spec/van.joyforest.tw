A campervan is not for rushing—it is a mobile living space

Take it slow. Leave time for staying put and living in the moment.

Campervan travel works best when you take it slow. We do not recommend driving too far each day or stacking too many sights. Plan 1–2 main stops per day and leave time for cooking, resting, walks, photos, and the view.

Not sure where to start? Try North Coast 3-day 2-night itinerary, Beginner 3-day 2-night slow trip, or Seaside van-life inspiration. For pricing and the van, see Price & Van.
