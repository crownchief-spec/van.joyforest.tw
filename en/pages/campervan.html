Why we recommend 3 days 2 nights

Campervan travel is not like a regular car rental—you need time to learn the systems, plan stops, park at campsites, and enjoy outdoor life. With only 2 days 1 night, the schedule often feels rushed and incomplete.

So our main package is 3 days 2 nights, giving you enough time to plan and actually feel what makes campervan travel fun.
