## When the van parks in the dark: timelapse to hold the whole sky

At camps or coast with **low light pollution and open views**, day is sea and mountains; night becomes **Milky Way and dense stars**, wind and waves in the distance. "Putting your bedroom under the stars" is a memory many renters never forget.

Night timelapse compresses what the eye slowly absorbs: **cloud drift, star trails, Milky Way sliding overhead**—like a moving postcard for the trip.

## Want to stargaze from the van too?

Besides outdoor camp, many layouts let you **lie on the cab-over bed or skylight** and watch the sky (see [Stargazing lying down: skylight, AC, and night sky from bed](/en/trip-stories/stargazing-sunroof/)). Before you go, confirm trip length, campsite type, and door-to-door delivery, then pick a route with room to slow down.
