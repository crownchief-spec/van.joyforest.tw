## Trip story｜Campervan to Wuling Farm: steady mountain driving, forest comfort at camp

High mountains aren't off-limits—**drive steady, turn slow**, and many guests put **Wuling Farm** on a mountain line: climb, pass through mist, watch forest change; the van is your **mobile base**. At camp or beside a deck pitch, skip the big tent—connect shore power, table, chairs, small lights—**photos, timelapse, clouds and night sky** into memory and album.

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>The climb is quite "campervan friendly."</strong> This van is built for long trips and varied roads—follow limits and corner rhythm, mind oncoming traffic and shoulder space, and you bring beds and kitchen to higher elevation. When it turns cold and branches wear rime or thin fog, the window looks like silver fairyland—then you're glad it's not a tent: <strong>step inside, close the door, turn on heat</strong>.</p>

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>At camp, the goal is "ease into the mood."</strong> Park, open side and rear doors, warm bulbs on deck and net shelter, hot food on the table; blue-hour outside, <strong>heat/AC and familiar layout</strong> inside—kids add layers, adults serve soup, no one queues in wind for shared facilities. That's what people mean: <strong>campervan travel brings comfort into the view</strong>, not willpower against weather.</p>

<div class="callout"><strong>Note</strong>: Wuling Farm and highland roads—<strong>entry, traffic control, weather, and road conditions</strong> change often; check latest notices before departure; campervan access, parking, and pitch types per <strong>on-site rules and booking/handover briefing</strong>.</div>
