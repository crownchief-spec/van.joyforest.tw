## Trip story｜Taipei 101 pickup to North Coast dinner, overnight, and morning light

Want **city night views** and **coastal sunrise** in one trip? Three photos sketch a very believable campervan mini-journey: pickup downtown, slow along the North Coast; seafood dinner to close the day; waves as background at night; next morning, roll over and meet sunrise and surf through the window.

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>① City departure, full ceremony.</strong> Handover near Xinyi at dusk, Taipei skyline beside the van—like telling yourself: "For the next few days, home moves with the sea breeze." Stock groceries and drinks on the way; the fridge becomes a mobile pantry so cooking later isn't frantic.</p>

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>② North Coast seafood dinner, eaten slowly.</strong> North along the sea line—harbor snacks, seafood stir-fry, or simple noodles work as "night-one landing ritual." After eating, don't rush; find a <strong>legal, comfortable-view</strong> coastal spot or campsite, pull out table and chairs, or stay inside with a small light and sort photos to distant waves.</p>

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>③ Stars and surf—the "late-night room type" only a campervan gives.</strong> Coastal nights are often clearer than the city: wind outside the door, familiar bed and bathroom inside. That freedom of **traveling with your room** is why many rent once and stay hooked—the image below is the mood of parking into the sea breeze after dark.</p>
