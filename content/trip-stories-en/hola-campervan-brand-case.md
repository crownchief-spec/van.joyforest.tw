## Case study｜HOLA used a campervan as SNOW TOUCH ad & pop-up base

Home brand **HOLA** combined a campervan with the **SNOW TOUCH cool-touch bedding line** for **outdoor brand photography** and a **physical pop-up**: white and light-blue graphics on the van—both **mobile showroom** and **step-in comfort space**.

In a city plaza, the theme was "**Heat-Relief Station**": large white tent, brand backdrop, cool-product display and check-in desk, van at the center of foot traffic. Staff guided visitors to **queue, tour, and feel the interior**—"bring natural cool home" as a **touchable, walk-in mobile base**; stills showed grass outside, window interaction, cab-over skylight, and in-van cool bedding in **light camping / road-trip** settings.
