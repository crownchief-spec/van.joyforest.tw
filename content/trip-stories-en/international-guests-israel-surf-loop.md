## How international guests travel｜Three Israeli friends, seven-day loop: surf, roads, mobile cabin

Sometimes the best trip is **not rushing checkpoints** but **having something to do every day**. Below is an **itinerary story** inspired by a real handover: three guys pick up in central Taipei, load boards and bags into a **mobile base**, loop Taiwan along coast and mountains—**foreigners traveling freely in Taiwan** on a mates' road trip.

### Seven days? A "mates surf loop" script

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>Day 1｜Taipei, pickup day.</strong> Handover near <strong>Regent Taipei</strong>; treat the van as a <strong>big luggage bay</strong>: wetsuits, boards, supplies aboard. Don't force distance—evening along <strong>North Coast or Northeast Coast</strong> for a windy parking spot; night-one instant noodles in the van still count.</p>

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>Day 2–3｜Yilan, Hualien—the sea owns the clock.</strong> Time for <strong>Wushi Harbor, Waiao</strong> breaks and coastal highway; when tired, <strong>Qingshui Cliffs, Qixingtan</strong> for staring and photos. Campervan upside: after surfing, no hotel rush—rinse salt, then decide on night market.</p>

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>Day 4–5｜Taitung, Kenting—tan the south.</strong> Drift <strong>Highway 11</strong> or aim for <strong>Hengchun Peninsula</strong> waves; Nanwan, Jialeshui—"how's the swell today" debates; one van, one co-pilot watching waves, one shouting hungry. Night van-camping by the sea; compare snores to engine rumble.</p>

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>Day 6｜Head west, snacks as victory lap.</strong> Detour <strong>Tainan</strong> for beef soup and squid noodles, or <strong>Chiayi</strong> turkey rice—last few hundred km, taste Taiwan again.</p>

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>Day 7｜Back Taipei, return van, keep the stories.</strong> Wash, fuel, shake out sand; phone full of "mates selfie + waves" and "open door, there's the sea." Not luxury, but <strong>very free</strong>—guys' trip doesn't need a tour bus; with a campervan you bring living room and beds around the island.</p>
