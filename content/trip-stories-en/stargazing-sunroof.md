Many assume stargazing means grass or open camp—but in a campervan you can do something romantic: **lie flat, AC on, look straight up through the skylight**. The cab-over bed sits under a panoramic roof: clouds and treetops by day, constellations at night without heat and bugs outside. (The cover image above is a real night skylight shot.)

## Why the skylight matters

First, **simple flow**: after a full day, shower or wipe down, climb into bed—no chairs dragged outside for an angle. Second, **comfort**: summer nights and coastal camps are hot and buggy; **AC and a sealed cabin** relax adults and kids—kids often stare at the glass and fall asleep fast. Third, **privacy and safety**: doors closed, outdoor feel without being on full display in a public pitch.

## Practical tips

Operate the skylight per handover instructions; **close and latch in thunderstorms or high wind**. For photos, dim interior lights to cut glass reflection. Haven't rented yet?
