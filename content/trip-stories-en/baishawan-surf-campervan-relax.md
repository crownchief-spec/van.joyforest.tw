## Guest experience｜Tired from the sea, perfect in the van

**Friends out together**—simple goal: **Baishawan, Taipei** area to surf, swim, and enjoy water. Big sun, enthusiastic waves; ashore with salt and sand, hearts full.

<p class="intro-lead"><strong>With the van as base, you own the rhythm.</strong> No rush for public showers or wet hair in a restaurant; open the door to your space, shower, dry clothes, <strong>AC on</strong>, sit and talk—that "finally cool" relief is the most healing stretch of this coastal mini-trip.</p>
